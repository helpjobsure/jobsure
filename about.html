About JobSure Automation Training

We deliver industry-ready automation training — practical projects, lab sessions and placement support to launch careers in automation.

Who We Are

JobSure Automation Training is a focused institute for Industrial Automation training. Our programs cover PLC, SCADA, DCS, HMI and AutoCAD Electrical with hands-on labs and real industrial projects. We bridge the gap between academics and industry by training students for core engineering roles and supporting them through placements.

We run online and offline batches, provide internships, and help students with resume preparation and interview training.
